Website (but... you're already there...)

I originally made my website during the winter of 2024 for High Seas, one of Hack Club's flagship YSWS's (You Ship We Ship (meaning you make something and they send you free stuff (pretty great deal! (have you ever seen this many nested parentheses? (maybe if you write in Lisp (will I ever learn Lisp? (it sounds intriguing, but probably not (Lex likes it, so maybe I should learn it someday (anyways, I digress))))))))).

Well, now that I'm thinking about High Seas, I need to rant a little about YSWS's.

My rant

Hack Club is great. They're a non-profit, they have lots of money, they give out lots of free stuff. When I did High Seas, I got more free stuff than I know what to do with. In fact, I have about $200 in Cloudflare credits through a grant from Hack Club. I'll be able to keep my domain registered for about another 10 years.

I'm extremely grateful for all of that!

But, I don't really do YSWS's (or almost anything Hack Club related) anymore. The reason being: my personal goals are not lined up to what they like to see.

Hack Club likes web, and web only. They like projects that you can make fast and ship fast.

That was great for me a few years ago (and again, I'm not trying to hate on Hack Club or anything, I'm really just ranting), but now it doesn't work for me. Something about myself is that I don't like web development (working on this website is fun and enjoyable, but anything beyond that bothers me. too many "full stacks" and all that bloat).

Instead, I want to pour my heart and soul into projects like Endian, which I can actually optimize and make neat and tidy.

Unfortunately, multi-year long projects don't sit well with YSWS's. Even when I broke up Endian into smaller parts by writing libraries like registry.c or Strint, they told me that I couldn't submit C libraries as a project.

What??? How are libraries not considered projects?

Ok, rant over.

So, I made my website, made some nice CSS, and wrote about my projects. I didn't write a lick of Javascript though, which I am proud to say.

About a few months before writing this, however, my dad told me it looked too childish?!?!? (maybe because I am a child!).

Anyways, I remade it too look like what you are seeing right now. It really reminds me of one of the last scenes of Stepbrothers, when they are at that gathering; Will Ferrel and his stepbrother were talking to each other about taxes and colonoscopies or something.
