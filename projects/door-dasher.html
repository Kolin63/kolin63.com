Itch

I have a lot of great memories from working on this. This was my first ever game entry, my first ever Godot project, and my first project which I would actually consider a full project. I made it with my friend Ayan Desai in 9th grade.

This was made for the GMTK 2024 game jam, which is now the biggest jam in the world! If you don't know what a jam is, it's a competition where you must make a game in a short amount of time (4 days for GMTK) based on a theme. After development, everybody plays some other games and gives them a rating. The cool thing about GMTK is that there are so many entries that it is impossible to play even a large amount of them.

The theme for GMTK 2024 was "What's behind that door?". After some brainstorming, Ayan and I came up with the idea of following a Doordash employee. They travel to three different customers, and there is always a weird person behind the door.

I learned a lot from this, whether it be about collaborating with others, using the Godot engine, programming, or art.

If you want to play the game, you can do so on Itch.
