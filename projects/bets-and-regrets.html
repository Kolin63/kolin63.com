This was a very unique experience for me--getting to work on a four-person team on a game over the timespan of 12 hours. Also, winning 2nd place in the global competition was a lot of fun and huge honor for us!

Pre-Event

I found out about Counterspell in the fall of 2024 when an organizer from Hack Club reached out to the advisor of my high school's computer club. I had known about Hack Club, the non-profit organization for high schoolers, for some time, but wasn't able to participate in any events until I became a freshman. So, when the announcement got sent out to the Computer Club, I was excited to jump at the opportunity!

With a few weeks until the event, I coordinated car rides with my friend Ayan, who I was going to team with. Also, I began to dust off my Godot skills so I could be ready for the 12-hour day.
