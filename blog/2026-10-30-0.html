Alright, here we go. I'm watching the new Life series, there's a quiche in the oven, and I don't have to go to sleep for a couple hours (state championships tomorrow!).

SO! There will not be a tile system, as I previously planned. There will still be a coordinate system, but that is not what the players will directly interact with.

Instead, there will be a POI system. Players list out the nearby POI's and choose which one to go to.

The coordinate system will use DMS. Here is a useful map to get an idea for the scale of that. Basically, New York City is in the size of one tile, which is great for the scale I am going for.

Each POI has different actions associated with it. For example, at a mine, you could mine for resources; in a forest, you can harvest wood; cities are hotspots for trading.

For now, I will be manually placing each POI on Earth. In the future, the players will be able to create their own (starting cities, mining outposts, terraforming planets, etc).

I'm still thinking over a major decision: whether or not their should be skill-based progression. I definitely don't want to have a jobs system like Adriencoin did, but skills would be something similar.

Ok, I just thought it over, and there will be skills. Level ups will make you better at your job and let you use better tools.
