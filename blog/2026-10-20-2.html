Blog 2026-10-20-2

Although I haven't had any time to work on Endian recently, I've been thinking about it a lot. There's a lot of ideas brewing in my head!

I don't really want to say this, but I might end up rewriting the reflection engine from scratch, or at least do a massive refactor of it. I was looking at the code the other day for the first time in at least a few weeks, and it is a complete mess. The main source of entropy is the multi-page long function bodies that parse text files for tags and whatnot. I'm not sure how I could refactor that, but I might do some research into other similar programs (like compilers or interpreters) to see how they do it.

I was looking at the code, by the way, because I wanted to add reflection line blames. As in, the reflector would append a comment to each line saying which file and mod they got that from. Similarly, I would like to add a feature to the reflector which can generate files based on the tag data. I'm not sure how feasible that is, especially because I would then have to make a more intricate cleanup system, but it might happen eventually.

What I really don't want to happen is for me to release this game, then end up wanting to make some big change to the engine, so I would have to take a break in content updates. Honestly, that sums up my entire design philosophy for the Endian engine.

Anyways, on the content side of things, I'm also considering doing a big refactor/rewrite of the base mod. Oh, I might also remove namespaces (again). I feel like they just add bloat and confusion. And entropy! Kill the entropy!

Curl's done compiling on my codespace. I'll finish writing my design ideas later. I do actually want to get them written down, so that they will hopefully be more coherent.
