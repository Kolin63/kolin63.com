I'm having a lot of fun playing Factorio.

I started a new save maybe a week or two ago, and I am loving it (as always). I am definitely playing with a lot more expertise than I was in my previous save, however long ago that was. I've already graduated from a spaghettified early game base to a bus-based mid game base, which has raw materials shipped in via train.

2.0's new train interrupt system is GOLDEN. I had to do a bit of circuitry to make it actually effective, but once everything was in place, the trains started moving around in such a satisfying way. It's so much fun to see them pick up jobs, fulfill them, then find a different one (based on a priority system that I made!)

I'm playing on the rail world preset, so biters are a problem, but not too big of one. Right now, my main priority is to get 30spm so that I can unlock artillery and take out nests without a trouble.

This has taken up the free time I could have been spending on Endian, but my brain needs to take a break sometimes, ok!
