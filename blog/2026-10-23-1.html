NJ passed a state law for this school year, banning all use of cell phones. Their specific wording is "internet enabled devices." Here is their FAQ PDF.

I have a Raspi 3 lying around. I have some dreams lying around too; could I surgically remove the wireless module and the ethernet port? That would make it not internet enabled, meaning that I could bring it to school.

Only thing is, if I wanted to use any graphical programs, the pi would crawl to a halt. And I think that I can do every shell based operation from the GitHub codespace which I am using right now.

Is it a cool idea? Definitely. Is it worth it? Maybe.
